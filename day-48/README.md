# DAY 48: Switching course

## Why I am switching from the MLH 100 Days of Solana to the 2026 Solana Developer Bootcamp
I have been doing the [100 Days of Solana Challenge](https://www.mlh.com/events/100-days-of-solana/challenges) for the past 47 days. I have learnt a lot and I am grateful for the time invested by the creator(s) of this project into making learning resources available to the masses.

However, the 100 days of Solana challenge is not scratching that itch. So, after careful consideration, I have decided to follow the [Solana Developer Bootcamp 2026](https://youtu.be/2pcm7ICRJKU?si=4Y2W2gIAwLx5UqtV).

I hope this change will be beneficial and will give me the knowledge to ship real applications.

Wish me luck!
